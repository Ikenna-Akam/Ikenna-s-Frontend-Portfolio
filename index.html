About Me

I'm Ikenna Emmanuel Akam, a frontend developer who enjoys the process of turning an idea or a design file into something people can actually use in a browser. I care as much about how an interface feels to navigate as I do about how the code behind it is structured.

Most of my time is spent working with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, translating layouts into responsive interfaces that hold up across screen sizes. I pay close attention to spacing, hierarchy, and the small details that make a page feel considered rather than assembled.

I'm equally comfortable exploring the design side of a project, from laying things out in Figma to putting together supporting visuals in Canva, which helps me understand a design's intent before I start building it.
